## Defect found and fixed

- **`validate_rag` could not pass on a free-tier Gemini key.** It ingests twice
  back to back; with 67 chunks that is 134 embedding requests, over the
  100/min free-tier limit, so the second `/ingest` returned 500
  (`GoogleGenerativeAIError`, 429). Added `--pause-seconds` (default 0, so
  behaviour is unchanged unless set) to wait between the two ingests.
