## Handoff limits observed

- **Groq free-tier rate limits.** Back-to-back runs hit 429 on synthesis; the
  orchestrator correctly fell back to labelled sections (`[rag] …`), which the
  validator treats as a failure. Leave ~60s between validator runs, or use a paid key.
- **Gemini free tier:** 100 embedding requests/min; a full `docs/` ingest is 67.
- **GitHub unauthenticated:** 60 requests/hour; add an approved read-only token.
- **Router phrasing.** "Explain the runbook procedure for firing alerts…" was
  routed to RAG only (no Monitoring); asking which alerts *are firing* routes
  to Monitoring. "Database connections" also routes to Infra (RDS), which fails
  without AWS.
- **Monitoring pod data is synthetic.** `pod-metrics` is the demo exporter
  (2 pods), so Monitoring and Infra (10 real pods) disagree; synthesis flags it.
- **Stale orchestrator consumer groups.** Each orchestrator start creates
  `orchestrator-<id>`; old groups keep lag on `opsbrain.agent.replies` until
  Kafka's offset retention expires them. Harmless locally.
- The Infra kubeconfig token expires after 24h (regenerate per OPU-102).
